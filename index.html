Before the call

Common questions

Don't you just send more leads?

No — appointments, not leads. Every appointment is exclusive to you and pre-qualified against your minimum project size. One contractor per prospect. You're not bidding against three other trucks for the same kitchen.

What if we already get referrals?

Keep them — they're free, and they're working. This isn't a replacement for referrals; it's a second channel your crews can run when word-of-mouth goes quiet, so growth isn't tied to how many people happened to mention you this month.

How much does this cost?

Structure and investment are covered on the call, once we know your service area, project mix, and crew capacity — those inputs change the number. What we can say now: one kitchen or bathroom project typically covers several months of the program.

What if your leads don't show?

These are confirmed, reminded appointments — not names on a spreadsheet. That's part of what "exclusive" means: we're accountable for the appointment showing up, not just for handing you a name and number.

How long until we see results?

Most partners see their first qualified appointment inside two to three weeks. Pipeline visibility 30–60 days out is usually in place by month two, once your service area's demand data has a few weeks behind it.
